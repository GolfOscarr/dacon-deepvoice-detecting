# 05 — Pre-PR review: bugs and risks in the strategy-v1 pipeline

*Four independent read-only reviews of `feat/eda` at `ee23f2f` on 2026-09-24, one per slice
(draw · render/ship/cache · corpus/folds · audit and training seams). Every finding was
confirmed by running code against the built artifacts; the reviewers' scripts are not in the
repo. Nothing here has been fixed yet — this is the list the next step works from.*

Severity: **BUG** = wrong output or a leak, fix before training · **RISK** = a cue or a mismatch
the model can learn, decide before training · **SEAM** = missing or conflicting code between
`processing/` and `training/`, needed for integration · nit = documentation or hygiene.

## D · Nits

* Stale docs: `sampler.py:10-11` says `U(3, 8)`; 03's DRAW-3 rule block still describes offset `U(0, file−take)`, a capped take, a remainder tile, take 3/8 and floor 2.0; `_both_sides` docstring claims 7,900 cfad pairs; `corpus.py` docstring says "three departures" and lists four; `processing/cache.py` and 03:221 say the cache differs from `load_audio` "at each tile's edges < 2 % RMS" — it is a sub-sample phase offset over the whole slice (2–61 % RMS, the cache being the correct timeline); `docs/pipelines/05:22` lists "source offset" as an I1b feature that `training/audit.py:144` excludes; `scripts/filter_track_licences.py:20-24` still calls ND undecided.
* G-EDA1 will fail on every run: its 2,907 = 2,884 ND + 23 denied, and ND was ruled usable on 2026-09-08.
* `label_confidence` equals `file_fake` on every row ("exact" fake / "reported" real): a per-tier loss would read the label.
* The G7 rate divides a post-drop count by a pre-drop denominator (1,032 vs the worklist's 1,038). `_label_of` maps cells 5 and 8 only. The music caveat `[1, 2, 1, 1]` counts udio, never drawn, in fold 1.
* `rir` picks `rng.integers(8) % nch`, so channels 8–29 of the 16- and 30-channel responses are never used, and it re-reads the response file per call (~70 ms). The band filter is a circular brick wall (`models/audio.py:86-89`): file-end content wraps into the start at −17 dB in the first 100 samples; train/test symmetric.
* `config.py:247-293`: accepts a reversed `gain_db_range` (every gain clipped to −15), `gain_db_sigma < 0` and a NaN SNR range (fail at draw), a `duration_range` outside render's 4–60 (fails at I12), `db='loud'`, `telephone_hz 44100`, `container mp3_0`, `scheme_version 2`.
* Frame intervals: whole-file and cells 6/7 produce overlapping 'file' spans with different labels — the future rasteriser must take the max, not the last write.
* Performance: ~3.2 samples/s/core at 1 thread (cache read 15 ms, compose 4 ms, augments 160 ms — rir 70, rawboost 40, pink 34 — normalize 131 ms; mp3 spawns ffmpeg at 200–240 ms, 70 % of draws spawn it). Module state is fork-safe.

## What the reviews confirmed is sound

Cell mix within 0.004 of config; structure, crossfade, lead / tail, gain, layer rate and SNR ranges as specified; augment and normalize draws flat across labels (names and parameters); 480,452 tiles with 0 margin violations, 0 reads past a file, 0 past the timeline, tiles exactly `span/n`; one take per sample never capped by a file; files at exactly 4.0 s safe; DOSS shares within 0.0036 per domain; sample-exact placement on 2,112 placements, lead exactly zero, no gaps or overlaps; the cache bit-identical to the quantised full resample on stereo, 8- and 30-channel, 22.05 k and space-in-path files, 228,801 / 228,801 present; I14 bitwise for lengths 1 to 160,000 with mixed channels; the 7,200 Hz cut below the mp3 and resampler shelves; no grouping key, sha256 or domain key straddles a fold or PROBE; the in-memory fold rebuild is identical even with shuffled rows; `validate_manifest` passes; labels agree with paths in every corpus; D-14 applied exactly; verdict and manifest match; cache lengths match `duration_s` (fma mp3 12–24 ms short, inside the margin); the audit catches every *file_fake*-conditioned mutation tried (lead, take, rir, mp3, μ-law, gain, layer SNR, gain_jitter); the pooled 0.61 allowance is stratum-mediated by construction; `RenderedSample` shape and keys match the loop's expectations; the `to_dict` round trip is exact.
