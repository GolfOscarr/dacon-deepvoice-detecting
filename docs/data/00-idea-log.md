| 1 | Train a voice/instrumental **presence** detector first, agnostic to real vs AI | ✅ | Correct. AI voice still counts as voice present. Presence is a *composition* label — we get it free from how we build mixes. | [03](03-presence-data.md) |

| 5 | 'Simultaneous' = music with vocals → need fake-vocal / fake-instrument / fake-both songs | ✅ | Exactly right, and it's the 4-cell grid. Fake-both is obtainable from song generators; fake-one-only must be **composed by us from stems**. | [02](02-label-taxonomy.md), [05](05-synthesis-plan.md) |
| 6 | 'In sequence' handled by **augmented/concatenated** data | ✅ | Correct and necessary — the rules explicitly say 순차적으로. ⚠️ must also concatenate REAL-REAL, or splicing becomes a "fake" shortcut. | [06](06-augmentation-spec.md) |
| 7 | Augment: **pitch changes** | ⚠️ | Pitch shift is itself a resynthesis operation — it can imprint vocoder-like artifacts on REAL audio and create label noise. Published evidence: pitch shift costs MusicDET **+40 EER points**. Use as *inoculation* (pitch-shifted REAL stays REAL) at low rate, never as a generic multiplier. | [06](06-augmentation-spec.md) |
| 8 | Augment: **add background noise** | 🔧 | Yes — but ⚠️ **adding *music* as noise flips `MUSIC_PRESENT` to 1.** Use MUSAN's noise/speech partitions for noise; if music is added, update the label. | [06](06-augmentation-spec.md) |
| 9 | **CutMix-style** mixing of fake and real clips of the same type | 🔧 | Good instinct — it's the PartialSpoof setting, which is well studied (0.77% EER with multi-resolution labels). But **concatenate in time, don't superimpose**: overlaying real+fake voice masks the very artifacts we want. Label: any generated component present → that component is FAKE. | [06](06-augmentation-spec.md) |
| 10 | **Concat different types**: voice-instrument, voice-instrument-voice, 3+ segments | ✅ | Directly matches the 순차적 case. Add crossfades and vary segment counts. Apply label-independently. | [06](06-augmentation-spec.md) |
| 11 | Synthetic voice via **TTS with many source voices** | ✅ | Yes. Prioritize *number of distinct generator families* over hours per family. | [05](05-synthesis-plan.md) |
| 12 | Synthetic music via **AI music services (Suno etc.)** | 🔧 | Use **open-weight** generators instead (ACE-Step Apache-2.0, YuE, MusicGen, Stable Audio Open, AudioLDM2). Commercial ToS typically forbid using outputs to build detectors, and we could not ship the files. | [05](05-synthesis-plan.md), [01](01-rules-check.md) |
| 13 | **Paired real/fake sharing the same script** (e.g. "I like AI" spoken and TTS'd) | ✅⭐ | The strongest idea in your list. Removes text and speaker as confounds so the model must key on synthesis artifacts. This is how ASVspoof is designed. Extend it to music via caption-matched prompts. | [05](05-synthesis-plan.md#tier-s) |
| 14 | Use **DPO** or similar on those pairs | 🔧 | DPO is for aligning *generative* policies to preferences; we're training a discriminator. The right tool with the same spirit is a **pairwise margin-ranking / supervised-contrastive loss** on matched pairs — and it aligns directly with EER, which is a pure ranking metric. | [05](05-synthesis-plan.md#using-the-pairs-in-training) |
| 15 | **Mel-spectrogram analysis** to find shared AI patterns | 🔧 | Do it, but ⚠️ at 16 kHz the classic mel-band fingerprints (bitrate, 24→48 kHz upsampling, >8 kHz rolloff) are already destroyed. Point the EDA at what *survives* band-limiting. | [07](07-eda-plan.md) |
| 16 | **Focus on weak points** — artifact frequency ranges, pronunciation | ✅ | Yes, and it doubles as 2nd-stage report material (결과 해석 및 일반화, 15 pts). Concrete feature list in the EDA plan. | [07](07-eda-plan.md) |

| — | *(added)* **Every augmentation must be applied independently of the label** | ✅⭐ | The most common data-construction bug. If only fakes get concatenated/noised/pitched, the model learns the augmentation, not the artifact. | [06](06-augmentation-spec.md#-the-governing-rule) |
