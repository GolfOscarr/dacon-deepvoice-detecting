# 04 — Pool D: Fake Instrumental

**12.0 GiB, 1 source.** `fakemusiccaps` (zenodo-15063698) — 27,605 clips from **5 text-to-music
models**, CC BY-NC 4.0, verified at origin 2026-09-10. Target ~45 h.

🔴 **SONICS is not in this pool, and neither is CtrSVDD.** Both landed on 2026-09-11 and both were
described as fake-music sources. CtrSVDD is fake *singing* — voice by the rules, pool B
([02](02-pool-b-fake-voice.md)). SONICS is whole AI **songs**: its own `fake_songs.csv` reports
`no_vocal = False` for all 49,074 rows, so it is **cell 8**, not a component pool at all
([D7](#d7---sonics-the-corpuss-first-whole-file-source--tier-s) below). **Pool D is still
FakeMusicCaps alone, and its five families still sit below the ≥8 floor.**

🔴 **Read this first.** Pool D carries **0.27** of the metric — more than voice — and until
2026-09-10 it was empty. Both [`PROGRESS.md`](../../PROGRESS.md) and
[data/12](../data/12-acquisition-status.md) still say *"pool D has no acquisition path"*; that is
**stale**. FakeMusicCaps is in the bucket with a complete provenance record, and its own note calls
it *"the ONLY Pool D candidate in the whole inventory whose fake side is self-contained."*
It is also **the entire pool**: one source, five families, no redundancy. Every EDA finding here
lands on an undiversified base, so this pool is measured at **100%**, not sampled
([00 §5](00-harness.md)).

## D1 — 🔴 Archive inventory and the real-half exclusion — Tier S, blocking

**Compute.** Unpack `FakeMusicCaps.zip` and enumerate the directory tree without ingesting
anything. Identify which directories hold each of the 5 TTM models' output, and identify **whether
the archive also ships the MusicCaps real audio** (or references to it). Produce an explicit
allow/deny list of directories.

**Why it is meaningful.** The acquisition record's caveat is unambiguous: *"Take the FAKE half
only. FakeMusicCaps is MusicCaps re-generated by TTM models: the generated audio is the authors' to
license, but MusicCaps itself is YouTube-derived and [data/01](../data/01-rules-check.md) rules
that out. If the download carries a real-audio half, it does not enter the corpus."* Ingesting a
YouTube-derived real half would put non-redistributable audio into a corpus we are **required to
ship to DACON** — a licence failure, not a modelling one, and the one class of mistake that cannot
be fixed after the fact.

**What we discover.** The exact per-model directory layout, which *is* the `artifact_family` key
and the DOSS `domain_key` for the whole pool.

**Gate.** **G-EDA1** — no pool-D row's path resolves under a MusicCaps real-audio directory.
Assert it in the corpus builder, not in a review.

---

## D2 — 🔴 Per-model census: duration, sample rate, container — Tier S

**Compute.** Over 100% of pool D: clip count, duration histogram, native sample rate, channel
count, codec, per model. Then the **joint** table against pool C's
([03 C5/C6](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md)).

**Why it is meaningful.** Two confounds converge here, both with 0.27 riding on them:

1. 🔷 **Duration.** MusicCaps is a corpus of **10-second** clips, so FakeMusicCaps is very likely
   uniformly ~10 s. Pool C is **30-second** excerpts. If that holds, `duration_s` alone separates
   `MUSIC_FAKE` almost perfectly — and the repo has a documented precedent for exactly this trap:
   *"the FoR dataset ships a `for-2sec` variant purely to eliminate duration-vs-label bias"*
   ☆ `[FoR dataset]`, recorded as E-A7 in [data/07](../data/07-eda-plan.md).
2. 🔷 **Native sample rate.** The five TTM families do not share one. AudioLDM-class models
   generate at 16 kHz; MusicGen at 32 kHz; Stable-Audio-class at 44.1 kHz. So *within* pool D,
   native rate identifies the generator — which makes a generator-disjoint fold trivially
   separable for the wrong reason — and *between* pools it adds to the C/D confound.

**What we discover.** Whether either hypothesis holds, and the numbers to fix them with.

**What it changes.** A single crop policy over both music pools: **draw every music component at a
random offset with an identical crop-length distribution**, which neutralizes duration and onset
morphology ([C5](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md)) in one step. And a resample-then-codec pass so
native rate is not recoverable. ⚠️ Both are transforms — R2 makes them symmetric obligations, not
options.

## D3 — 🔴 Near-Nyquist profile per model, both planes — the cheap half of E-A1 — Tier A

**Compute.** Per TTM family: `ltas[128]`, `band_energy[8]`, `near_nyquist_ratio`,
`effective_bandwidth_hz` — at the **native** plane and at the **chain** plane. Then two separability
numbers: a linear probe over families at native, and the same probe at 16 kHz. Report the **drop**.

**Why it is meaningful.** E-A1 — the 16 kHz survivability probe — is named in
[PROGRESS](../../PROGRESS.md) as the **highest-value single experiment** in the project, and it is
specified as *train one small music-fake model on identical data at 48 kHz vs 16 kHz*. That is
days of work. This measurement is hours, and it answers the cheap half:

- ArtifactNet's Table XI reports AI residual bandwidth **~291 Hz** vs human **~1,996 Hz**, which
  *looks* like it should survive an 8 kHz Nyquist, yet the paper insists 44.1 kHz is required.
  That contradiction is what E-A1 exists to settle.
- A generator that produces at 16 kHz natively and one that produces at 44.1 kHz and gets
  downsampled leave **completely different** traces at our Nyquist — the second carries a resampler
  shelf, the first does not. Measuring the two planes separates "the artifact survives" from "the
  upsampling residue survives", and those have opposite implications: the first is a real signal,
  the second is a corpus-identity shortcut that will not exist in DACON's test set.

**What we discover.** How much of the per-family separability is spectral content above 8 kHz
(destroyed by the competition's own standardization) versus below it (real signal we can use).

**⚠️ Stated honestly: this is a screen, not a substitute for E-A1.** A linear probe over LTAS
summaries measures whether *these statistics* separate families; an SSL frontend may find structure
that they do not. A **collapse** at 16 kHz is strong evidence the premise is in trouble and should
trigger E-A1 immediately. **Survival is weaker evidence** and does not retire E-A1. Do not let a
green screen close the question.

**Output.** `d_family_separability.csv` (probe AUC at both planes), the LTAS overlay per family.

## D4 — Family-count shortfall, quantified against the fold builder ⚠️ Tier S

**Compute.** Assemble a manifest with pool D at 5 families and run `training.folds.build_folds`
feasibility at 5/4/3/2 folds. Then repeat with 6, 7, 8 synthetic families to find the **minimum
family count** that makes each fold count feasible while still permitting a sealed PROBE slice.

**Why it is meaningful.** [validation/01](../validation/01-split-scheme.md) argues the music-family
floor should rise from ≥5 to ≥8: *"at 5, a family-disjoint 5-fold puts one family per validation
fold and the sealed PROBE slice cannot be carved out at all, on the highest-weighted component
head."* FakeMusicCaps gives exactly **5**. So we are at the floor the validation design says is too
low, and the gap is currently an argument rather than a number.

**What we discover.** The exact number of additional generator families ACE-Step / YuE /
Stable Audio / MusicGen must supply — which is a GPU-hours budget line, not a preference.

**What it changes.** The Phase-B generation plan's size and priority.
⚠️ The smoke corpus's pool D had *8* spoofed generators and still failed, because they were
**environmental**: *"the count is satisfied and the domain is not."* Count is necessary, not
sufficient — the new families must be **music**.

## D5 — Caption / prompt census, matched against pool C 🔴 Tier A

**Compute.** FakeMusicCaps inherits MusicCaps' captions — recover them from the archive metadata or
by joining on MusicCaps ids. Extract genre, instrumentation and mood terms; build the distribution.
Hold it against pool C's genre census ([C4](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md)) and report the
divergence plus the overlapping region.

**Why it is meaningful.** This is the other end of the C4 argument, and it is the finding with the
largest expected value in either music pool. The prompts *are* the generation distribution: if
MusicCaps captions skew toward instrumentation and genres that FMA/Jamendo do not contain, genre
predicts `MUSIC_FAKE` for reasons unrelated to synthesis — a 0.27-weight confound invisible to
every spectral check in this plan.

**What we discover.** The overlap, and a **prompt list for pool-D generation**: the pool-C genre
tags underrepresented in FakeMusicCaps are exactly what ACE-Step should be prompted with, which
both closes the confound and adds families (**D4** above).
Two problems, one job.

**What it changes.** The generation prompt distribution, and a possible cap on over-represented
genres in both pools.

## D6 — Degenerate-generation screen ⚠️ Tier A (F-S4)

**Compute.** The [B4](02-pool-b-fake-voice.md) screen, applied to music: silence, duration
outliers, mel-envelope autocorrelation for looping, `statistics_T` below the 0.8 quantile **of its
own family**, and a flat-spectrum check (some TTM failures emit noise).

**Why it is meaningful.** Text-to-music models fail visibly and often — a silent clip labelled
`MUSIC_FAKE = 1` teaches the 0.27-weight head that silence is fake. With only five families, a
single family's failure rate is 20% of the pool's diversity.

**⚠️ Two cautions specific to music.** Long stretches of near-silence are **legitimate** in music
in a way they are not in speech, so a fixed `rms_dbfs` floor will over-flag; threshold on the
file's own distribution. And *repetition* is a normal musical property — the looping detector must
run at lags long enough to distinguish a bar-level repeat from a stuck generation. Calibrate both
against pool C, where the same statistics are computed and the label is REAL: **any threshold that
flags pool C at a materially different rate than pool D is an asymmetric filter (R2) and is
manufacturing a cue.**

## 🔴 D8 — S tier: **every file is exactly 10.000 s**, and that is the corpus's sharpest shortcut

Measured 2026-09-14 over all 27,605 decoded files (`eda report`).

| p05 | median | p95 | in 4–60 s | under 4 s | over 60 s |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| **10.000** | **10.000** | 10.242 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |

Not "about ten seconds" — p05, median and p95 agree to three decimals, because the five text-to-music
models were each asked for 10 s clips. Pool C, the real instrumental it is scored against, has a
median of **30.003 s** ([03 C8](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md)).

🔴 **So `music_fake` is readable off duration with no audio at all**, and
[06 X1d](06-cross-pool.md#-x1d--x1-on-the-decoded-audio-the-duration-shortcut-survives-the-chain)
measures it: `duration_s_decoded` scores **0.946** univariate on the chain plane, and the pooled
`music_present` head holds 0.852 even after a whole publisher is held out. This pool carries
**0.27** of the metric.

⚠️ Resampling cannot touch it. The 16 kHz chain changes a file's rate, not its length, so this is a
shortcut that survives every render-time fix the harness has. It is a **sampler** problem — the
crop policy has to make pool C and pool D indistinguishable in length — and the test set is 4–60 s
for both.

Two smaller findings from the same pass:

* **Native bandwidth is already 8,000 Hz**, so the chain removes nothing (`bandwidth_lost_hz`
  0.000, `hf_ratio_8k_native` 6.7e-07). Whatever generator fingerprint exists here is inside the
  band the model sees — which is the good news.
* **DC offset reaches 0.356** and 5.7% of files clip. Worth a render-time high-pass, and worth
  checking before any level normalisation is chosen.

## D9 — Content evidence: 3.6% of the generated "instrumental" has speech in it

Run 2026-09-15 over all 27,605 files. Silero finds a speech ratio ≥ 0.20 in
**1,006 of them (3.6%)**, against an asserted `voice_present = 0`; median 0.000,
p90 0.016.

Lower than pool C's 12.6% ([03 C2b](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md)) and not
nothing: five text-to-music models asked for instrumental clips produce
vocal-like output some of the time. The same lower-bound caveat applies — sung
output is invisible to a speech detector.

⚠️ Worth pairing with [D8](#-d8--s-tier-every-file-is-exactly-10000-s-and-that-is-the-corpuss-sharpest-shortcut): the pool is
already separable from pool C by duration alone, so a mislabelled 3.6% is a
second-order problem here. It matters for the **presence** heads rather than for
`music_fake`.

## D7 — 🔴 SONICS: the corpus's first whole-file source — Tier S

**30.4 GiB, 49,074 tracks, 1,971 h, CC BY-NC 4.0.** Suno (25,426) and Udio (23,648). Acquired
2026-09-11 as `pool: D`; registered in [`configs/eda.yaml`](../../configs/eda.yaml) as
`row_kind: whole_file, cell: 8`.

**Compute.** Join `fake_songs.csv` onto the enumerated audio by `filename`, and census: `no_vocal`,
`source`, `algorithm`, `duration`, `bit_rate`, `genre`/`mood`/`topic`, and the authors' own
`split`. Then hold the duration and bitrate distributions against pool C's
([C5](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md), [C6](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md)).

**Why the pool assignment was the whole finding.** `POOL_LABELS["D"]` is
`voice_present=0, music_present=1, voice_fake=None, music_fake=1`. `CELL_TABLE[8]` is
`1, 1, 1, 1`. As pool D, 49,074 AI songs that **all** contain singing would have told the model
there is no voice present, routed sung vocals into the 0.27-weight music-fake head, and left
`voice_fake` null on the strongest fake-vocal evidence in the corpus. `validate_manifest` requires
`pool = null` on a whole-file row — *"a whole file is used as-is, not drawn from a component pool"*
— so the correction is not a relabelling, it is a change of row kind.

**🔴 What it unlocks, and the trap that comes with it.** The smoke corpus had **zero** whole-file
rows; every one of its 2,177 was a component. [data/02](../data/02-label-taxonomy.md)'s composition
trap needs non-composed examples in cells 5 and 8, or "composed" predicts the label — and SONICS is
the first source that can supply cell 8 honestly.

But the rule is an **equality**: `f_c` must match across cells on both sides of `FILE_FAKE`. 49k
whole-file cell-8 rows with no whole-file cell-5 counterpart makes **"is a whole song" predict
FAKE** — the same trap, mirrored. The counterpart is FMA/Jamendo tracks *with vocals*, which is
exactly what [C2](03-pool-c-real-instrumental.md)'s VAD sweep finds. **C2 is therefore
load-bearing rather than hygiene**, and MUSDB18-HQ — real full songs, cleared and unacquired —
is the source that would close it properly.

**Measured facts worth carrying.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Vocals | `no_vocal = False` on **49,074 of 49,074**. There is no instrumental subset to salvage for pool D |
| Artifact families | **2** (`source`: suno, udio). The 5 `algorithm` strings are versions — `chirp-v2/v3/v3.5` are all Suno, which is the model-name-vs-family distinction [validation/01](../validation/01-split-scheme.md) splits on |
| Hours | **1,971 h** against a ~45 h budget — **44×**. DOSS caps per domain; `source × algorithm` is the key |
| Duration | 32.9–240.1 s, median **131.2 s**. The first source reaching the competition's upper range — and mostly **above** its 60 s ceiling, so cropping is mandatory |
| Codec | ~**37 kbps** VBR mp3 against FMA's typical 128–320 |
| Split | The authors' `train`/`valid`/`test.csv` ship with it. Build our folds *from* them or a song leaks between folds |

🔴 **The bitrate is sharper than the acquisition caveat says.** That caveat warns about mp3 versus
MUSDB18-HQ's WAV; the live problem is mp3-versus-mp3, where ~37 kbps against 128–320 kbps is
trivially separable and `bit_rate` is a column [X1](06-cross-pool.md) reads directly. Whether the
artifact survives the 16 kHz chain is a two-plane measurement ([00](00-harness.md)); whether it
*should* is not a question — the codec stage has to equalise it either way.

⚠️ **The fake half only.** The 48,090 real songs ship as `real_songs.csv` — YouTube IDs, no audio —
and [data/01](../data/01-rules-check.md) rules YouTube-sourced audio out. Do **not** reconstruct
them. The config's `suffixes` excludes `.csv`, so the metadata files are not enumerated as audio.
