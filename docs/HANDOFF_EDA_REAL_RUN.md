### 🔴 Diagnosis 2 — X1 cannot verify its own fix, and must not be expected to

**This is the most important structural point in the handoff.** `X1` reads `files.parquet`, which
describes the corpus *as it sits on disk*. Those files really are 10 s and 30 s. The three
transforms above act at **render** time, on what the sampler draws.

So **`G-EDA2` will keep reporting 1.000 no matter what is fixed**, and chasing it to 0.5 is chasing
a number that cannot move. X1's job is done: it named the columns. Confirming they are closed is a
different measurement over the **spec stream** — `training.audit.audit_specs`, which is
[`EDA/06`](EDA/06-cross-pool.md) X5 — and that becomes the next gate.

⚠️ Do not "fix" this by deleting features from `METADATA_FEATURES`. That would make the gate green
while the corpus stayed confounded, which is the failure the gate exists to prevent.

## 4 — Consensus and standing instructions

**Commit to the branch whenever there is a solid advance.** Stage explicit paths — never
`git add -A`. Keep the repository's gates green (the pytest suite, `scripts/check_links.py`,
`scripts/mutate_eda.py`) before each commit, and follow the existing commit-message style:
a lowercase `area: what changed` subject, then prose explaining *why*, no bullet lists of files.

Repository specifics learned this session:

- **Branch is `feat/eda`**, and `main` stays where `origin/main` is. There is one worktree; do not
  create others. Commit the EDA to `feat/eda`; open a PR when a phase closes.
- **Never `git add -A`** — `eda/out/` is gitignored but the corpus paths are not in the repo at all,
  and `configs/eda.yaml` is under active edit.
- **`scripts/check_links.py` validates heading anchors**, computed with GitHub's algorithm. A
  heading containing an emoji keeps the surrounding spaces as hyphens, so anchors like
  `#d7---sonics-...` have three hyphens. Run it after every doc edit.
- **Every invariant is paired with a mutation observed to fail.** `scripts/mutate_eda.py` holds 37.
  Add to it when you add a guard; a guard with no mutant is not evidence.

From the user, this session:

- *"Doublecheck your plan correct/concrete"* — applied twice, and both times it found real defects
  that no test would have raised. **Check claims against the artifacts, not against memory.**
- *"Make dataset priority"* — wave order is by **label coverage, not size**: X1 is a per-head
  population statement and a one-class head reports `na`, so no gate can fire until both sides of
  all four heads are on disk.
- *"If there's an issue in EDA or bug, put them on document"* — hence the table in §3.

Decisions made and **not** to re-litigate:

- **SONICS is `row_kind: whole_file, cell: 8`, not pool D.** Its `fake_songs.csv` reports
  `no_vocal = False` for all 49,074 rows, and `POOL_LABELS["D"]` asserts `voice_present = 0`.
  `validate_manifest` requires `pool = null` on a whole-file row.
- **CtrSVDD is pool B, not a fake-music source.** The rules classify sung vocals as voice.
- **A filter is a distribution-shift decision; a transform is a symmetry obligation.** Fixes to the
  three confounds are **transforms applied to every pool**, never feature deletion.
- **Gates are a tri-state and `fail > na > pass`.** A check whose input is absent reports `na`.
  This was violated twice this session (`mtg-jamendo` vacuous pass, `cell8` consolidate) and fixed
  both times; expect it to recur wherever a new check meets an empty set.
